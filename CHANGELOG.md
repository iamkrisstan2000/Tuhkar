# Changelog

## 2.1.0 — consolidated hotpatch

- Floating, dimmed search with an animated, content-sized results dropdown in portrait and landscape.
- Enter/selection gracefully hides the field; Back exits search first; 15-second inactivity dismisses field/results. Landscape typing stays in the overlay.
- Corrected source-to-library spacing and compacted landscape Home; moved landscape Video source buttons beside its heading.
- Reused caches/recycled rows, removed the obsolete fixed-height sizing helper, purged cancelled search tasks and allowed idle background workers to time out.
- VersionName **2.1.0**, versionCode **77**: upgrades v2.0.0/code70 and internal hotpatch builds through 2.1.5/code76 with the existing signing identity.
